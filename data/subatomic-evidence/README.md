# Subatomic evidence cross-links

This directory holds one canonical experimental-observation registry shared conceptually between the Universe scale atlas and the nuclides atlas. Entries are **not nuclides** and must not be inserted into isotope tables.

- Experimental results and verification-pending references are explicitly distinguished.
- Cross-links: particles and fields; quantum optics; quasiparticles; condensed matter; spectroscopy.
- Keep energy resolution, physical length, mass, and temporal scales on separate axes.
- A bound state, superfluid, and supersolid are distinct classifications.
- Source URLs and DOI claims flagged for verification require primary-paper checks before elevation to verified.
- Data integration is additive: no existing visualization, data table or deployment is modified.

Registry: [observations.json](observations.json).
